This section covers how a computer works on the inside: how numbers and characters are stored as bits, how the processor executes instructions, which components a PC is made of and how the operating system manages processes, memory and files. It also covers the basics of theoretical computer science, which show what computers can and cannot compute in principle.

At the Austrian HTL for information technology, this material is mainly part of **systems engineering** (fundamentals of computer science, hardware and operating systems) in the first years and is extended in the higher years with operating system concepts and theoretical computer science. Number systems are covered in [Mathematics](/en/mathematics/algebra/number-systems/), the practical administration of Linux and Windows systems under [Deployment](/en/deployment/).
