At an HTL, history and civic (political) education are taught in the compulsory subject **"Geography, History and Civic Education"** (_Geografie, Geschichte und Politische Bildung_, GGP), together with geography and the basics of economics. It is based on the curriculum for Höhere Technische Lehranstalten (BGBl. II No. 262/2015), which distributes the content across semesters in a competence-oriented way: war and peace in the modern era, Enlightenment and revolutions, fundamental and human rights, industrialisation and technical development, colonialism and decolonisation, the contemporary history of the 20th and 21st centuries, parties, interest groups, media, elections, the European Union and security policy. The geography content can be found in a separate section.

The aim is for you to be able to explain historical developments, critically examine sources and accounts, judge political questions with reasons and take part in democracy yourself. The history pages are arranged chronologically and each contains a timeline. For the Reife- und Diplomprüfung, it is particularly important to place events in context and to relate them to the present.
