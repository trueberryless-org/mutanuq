Networking covers how computers exchange data: from cables and radio through switches and routers to the protocols on which the internet runs. In the HTL Information Technology programme, networking is a compulsory subject of its own in the first years and is continued in systems engineering and systems integration and infrastructure, where networks are planned and operated together with servers, security and the cloud. Many schools are Cisco Networking Academies and teach according to the three CCNA courses _Introduction to Networks_ (ITN), _Switching, Routing and Wireless Essentials_ (SRWE) and _Enterprise Networking, Security, and Automation_ (ENSA).

The pages follow the path of a data packet through the layers: first basic concepts and models, then from the physical layer up to the application, followed by network services, configuration and operation. The configuration examples use Cisco IOS, as in class with Packet Tracer.
