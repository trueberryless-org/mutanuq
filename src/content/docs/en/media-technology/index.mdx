Media technology is, alongside systems engineering, one of the two specialisations of the HTL for information technology (curriculum Annex 1.11). After the common foundation years, pupils deepen their knowledge of designing and producing digital media from the third year onwards. Depending on the school, the subjects are called media technology, media design or multimedia production, but the content is similar: the basics of perception and design, image, sound and video, the usability of websites and apps, 3D graphics and the law surrounding media.

This section combines technology and design. For each topic you will find the technical basics with formulas and worked examples (file sizes, data rates, contrast ratios) as well as the design rules needed in projects and for the diploma thesis. Where calculations are involved, there are programming examples in C# and Rust.
