The recommendation (Empfehlung) is an addressee-oriented, advisory text type written in the present tense and the present perfect. You present a subject (a book, a film, an exhibition, a product, a measure or a project), assess it against comprehensible criteria and give a specific target group reasoned advice. The decisive point is the **focus on the addressee**: a recommendation for your classmates looks different from a recommendation to the head teacher.

:::note[Matura]
Since the main exam date in 2020, the recommendation is no longer part of the catalogue of text types for the written SRDP in German. It is, however, still asked for in class tests and in working life (product recommendation, decision paper).
:::

## Characteristics

A recommendation has the following characteristics:

-   a clear addressee and a clear purpose (what should the reader do afterwards?)
-   short, factual information about the subject
-   assessment against criteria (for example content, quality, cost, benefit, effort)
-   advantages and disadvantages are weighed up, but the recommendation remains unambiguous
-   `Ich` (I) may be used, but the reasoning must be factual
-   addressing the reader (`du` or `Sie`, depending on the situation)
-   a heading that shows the subject and the direction
-   write at most 50 words more than required
-   short to medium-length text type

### Recommendation, review and commentary

| Text type                 | Focus                                                          |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Review (Rezension)        | critical discussion of a work for a broad readership           |
| Commentary (Kommentar)    | opinion on a current topic, often pointed                      |
| Recommendation (Empfehlung) | advice to a specific target group that is making a decision  |

## Structure

### Heading

The heading names the subject and already shows the direction of the recommendation.

> Ein Muss für alle Technikbegeisterten: das Ars Electronica Center in Linz
> (A must for all tech enthusiasts: the Ars Electronica Center in Linz)

### Introduction

Name the occasion and the subject with the most important data (title, author or provider, place, time). If the task refers to a source text, the basic sentence (Basissatz) goes here. Address the target group and arouse their interest.

> Ihr sucht noch ein Ziel für den Klassenausflug im Juni? Dann solltet ihr euch das Ars Electronica Center in Linz genauer ansehen.

### Main part

The main part consists of two parts:

1.  **Information:** Describe the subject briefly enough for the target group to get an idea of it (What is it about? What does it offer? What does it cost?).
2.  **Assessment:** Examine the subject against two to four criteria that matter to the target group. Treat each criterion in its own paragraph with a reason and an example. Mention weaknesses too; this makes your recommendation more credible.

> Besonders überzeugend sind die Mitmachstationen zu künstlicher Intelligenz: Man trainiert selbst ein kleines neuronales Netz und sieht sofort, warum es Fehler macht. Ein Nachteil ist der Eintrittspreis, der für Schulgruppen allerdings deutlich ermäßigt ist.

### Conclusion

In the conclusion, you summarise your verdict and state the recommendation clearly. You can qualify it (whom it applies to and whom rather not) and name a concrete next step.

> Ich kann euch den Besuch daher uneingeschränkt empfehlen. Plant aber mindestens drei Stunden ein und reserviert die Führung rechtzeitig.

## Useful phrases

| Purpose               | Phrase                                                                               |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| opening               | Wer ... sucht, sollte ... kennen. (Anyone looking for ... should know ...)           |
| introduce a criterion | Was den Inhalt betrifft, ... (As far as the content is concerned, ...)               |
| positive assessment   | Besonders gelungen ist ..., weil ... (Particularly successful is ..., because ...)   |
| name a weakness       | Ein Wermutstropfen ist ..., allerdings ... (One drawback is ..., however ...)        |
| weigh up              | Trotz dieser Schwäche überwiegen die Vorteile deutlich. (Despite this weakness, the advantages clearly outweigh it.) |
| recommend             | Ich empfehle Ihnen daher, ... (I therefore recommend that you ...)                   |
| qualified             | Für ... ist ... nur bedingt geeignet, da ... (... is only partly suitable for ..., since ...) |
| advise against        | Von ... würde ich eher abraten, weil ... (I would rather advise against ..., because ...) |
