Modern applications rarely run on a single server. Instead, they are distributed across many services that communicate with each other over the network, scale independently of each other and keep working even if individual parts fail. Such **decentralised** or distributed systems are nowadays mostly operated in the cloud.

This section uses **Microsoft Azure** as an example to show how such systems are built. However, the concepts can also be applied to other cloud providers such as AWS or Google Cloud.
