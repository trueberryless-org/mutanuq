**Stochastics** (probability and statistics) describes processes whose outcome depends on chance: component failures, transmission errors in networks, waiting times at a server or measurement errors. It combines probability theory, which derives predictions from a model, with statistics, which infers the model from data. The basis for describing data is [descriptive statistics](/en/mathematics/descriptive-statistics/).

At the HTL, stochastics is taught in the fourth and fifth year and is a fixed part of the standardised school-leaving and diploma examination (SRDP) in Applied Mathematics.
