**Economics** does not look at the individual company but at the economy as a whole: how do prices arise? Why is there inflation and unemployment? What does the European Central Bank do, and how is the state financed? Understanding these relationships lets you interpret economic news, assess political decisions and judge the conditions for your own business. The pages follow the "Economics and Law" curriculum at the HTL and use data for **Austria**.
