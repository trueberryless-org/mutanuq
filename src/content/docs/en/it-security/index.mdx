IT security protects information and IT systems against attacks, errors and failures. At the HTL for information technology it is a compulsory subject of its own in many timetables, running from the first to the fifth year, and at the same time it runs through network engineering, systems engineering, software development and the diploma thesis. The topics range from the mathematics behind encryption through secure programming and hardened servers to management systems and criminal law.

The pages build on each other: the basics clarify terms and threats, and cryptography provides the tools on which certificates, TLS and modern login methods are based. They are followed by the technical protective measures for web applications, networks and systems, and finally by the organisational and legal framework. Code examples are given in **C#** and **Rust**.
