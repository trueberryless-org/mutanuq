Geography studies how nature and people interact on Earth: how climate, rock and water shape landscapes, where people live and work, how goods and data flow around the world and why regions develop so differently. At the HTL it is part of the compulsory subject **Geography, History and Civic Education** (curriculum BGBl. II No. 262/2015), which is taught in years I to IV with two lessons per week each and also includes the fundamentals of economics. The historical and political part is in the section [History and Civic Education](/en/history-and-politics/).

The curriculum requires pupils to read and interpret maps, diagrams, images and texts, to use analogue and digital information systems, to explain geofactors and ecological interrelationships and to assess population development, economic regions and global interconnections. For IT specialists, geographic information systems, the physical infrastructure of the internet and the raw materials of IT are particularly relevant. All figures are given with year and source because they change constantly.
