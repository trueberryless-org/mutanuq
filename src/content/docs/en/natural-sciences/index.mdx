In the subject **Natural Sciences**, you learn the basics of physics, chemistry, and biology and ecology at the HTL. Physics provides the laws on which electrical engineering and computer science are built, chemistry explains materials, batteries and semiconductors, and biology and ecology show how technology, people and the environment are connected.

Formulas are given with SI units, and the worked examples are calculated with numbers. Where a process lends itself to simulation, such as a pendulum or the predator-prey model, there are additional programs in C# and Rust.
