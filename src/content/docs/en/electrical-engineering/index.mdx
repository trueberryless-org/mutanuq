Every computer, every router and every microcontroller is ultimately an electronic circuit. If you understand how voltage, current and components interact, you can connect hardware safely, narrow down faults, size power supplies and follow how transistors become logic gates and logic gates become processors.

At the HTL for information technology these topics are taught in the subject **Systemtechnik** (systems engineering, part electrical engineering and electronics), mainly in the first to third year: first DC circuits, measurement and passive components, then AC circuits, semiconductors, basic electronic circuits and digital technology. In the lab exercises you build circuits and measure them with a multimeter and an oscilloscope. Programming microcontrollers is covered in [Embedded Programming](/en/embedded-programming/).
