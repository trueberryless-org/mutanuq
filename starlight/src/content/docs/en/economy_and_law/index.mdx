Sooner or later, many HTL graduates become self-employed or take on responsibility in a company. Technical knowledge alone is not enough for this: you also have to understand how a company is founded, which legal framework applies and how to position products successfully on the market. The content refers to the legal situation in **Austria**.

## What to expect

- **Company foundation:** Business idea, personal and trade-law requirements, legal forms, founding steps and the business plan.
- **Marketing:** Marketing goals and strategies, the marketing mix, market research, the product life cycle and portfolio analysis.
