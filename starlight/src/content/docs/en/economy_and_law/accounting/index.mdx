**Accounting** records all cash flows and values of a company in figures. It shows whether a company is making a profit, whether it is solvent and at what price it has to sell its products. It is also the basis for taxes and for management decisions.

A distinction is made between **financial accounting** (bookkeeping and annual accounts, required by law and intended for the tax office, banks and owners) and **management accounting** (cost accounting and planning, used to steer the company).
