Law is everywhere – when shopping online, signing a mobile phone contract, starting your first job or running your own website. If you know the basics, you can conclude contracts correctly, enforce your rights and avoid mistakes that can become expensive. These pages follow the curriculum of the subject "Wirtschaft und Recht" (Economics and Law) at the HTL and refer to **Austrian law**.
