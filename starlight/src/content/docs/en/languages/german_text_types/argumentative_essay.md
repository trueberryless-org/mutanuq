The discussion essay is a neutral, objective text type with sophisticated argumentation, written in the present tense and the present perfect.

## Characteristics

A discussion essay has the following characteristics:

-   factual wording
-   neutral tone
-   creative introduction
-   summarising (and appealing) conclusion with your own opinion, final verdict
-   write at most 50 words more than required
-   heading possible
-   medium-length to long text type

## Structure

### Introduction

The introduction of a discussion essay should be as creative as possible. As an author, you want the readers to be captivated by the topic right away and to want to read the entire text. There are various methods for writing and starting a creative introduction:

-   start with a quote, something current or an experience
-   ask a rhetorical question to make the readers think

> Jeder kennt den Moment, wenn der Toast vom Tellerrand kippt und man weiß bereits vorher, dass er 100%ig auf der Marmeladenseite aufklatschen wird. Aber haben Sie sich jemals gefragt, warum dieses Ereignis so unvermeidlich ist?

> Als Aristoteles sagte, man könne den Wind nicht ändern, aber die Segel richtig setzen, ahnte er sicher nicht, dass sein Zitat einmal auf hunderten von Webseiten im Internet und in Büchern in Bibliotheken kursieren würde. Genauso wenig wissen wir heute, was in 200 Jahren sein wird.

### Main part

In the main part, the most important arguments relating to the topic are dealt with. Depending on whether you are supposed to write a pro and contra discussion or a supporting discussion (Belegerörterung), you deal with pro and contra arguments or only with arguments that reinforce each other. These arguments must always remain factual and must not include any personal judgement. It is also important to establish a clear reference to the source text.

### Conclusion

In the conclusion of a discussion essay, you briefly summarise the most important statements once more and discuss connections with possible other topics. In addition, you may be asked to write an appeal to the readers, which must also be done in the conclusion.
