The opinion speech is a subjective, argumentative, persuasive text type that is delivered in front of a specific audience. For this reason, all listeners must be addressed ([salutation](#salutation)).

The purpose of the opinion speech is to convince the audience of your own opinion by means of conclusive arguments. Depending on the audience, the opinion speech can be written formally (e.g. `Sie` / `Ihren`) or informally (e.g. `du` / `euren`). The opinion speech is written in the present tense and the present perfect and should not contain any insults or discrimination.

## Characteristics

-   `Ich` (I) should be used
-   convince the audience of my opinion (address the audience)
-   creative introduction that mentions the title and author
-   the main part argues fluently
-   summarising conclusion with an appeal
-   use [stylistic devices](/en/languages/german_text_types/stylistic_devices/)
-   write at most 50 words more than required
-   heading related to the situation (if required)
-   rather long text type; sometimes also medium-length

## Structure

### Heading

If the task requires a specific heading, it should be used.

### Salutation

The task describes a situation that explains the setting and the people in front of whom you deliver the opinion speech. From this situation, you have to infer or decide which people you want to and should address in the salutation. All genders must always be addressed if you do not know whether the audience consists exclusively of men or women. If a person of authority or of higher rank than yourself is in the audience, you must speak formally (`Sie` / `Ihren` / ...). If you know that only your friends are in the audience, you can (and it would probably make sense to) speak informally (`du` / `euren` / ...).

> Sehr geehrte Schülerinnen und Schüler!  
> Sehr geehrte Professorinnen und Professoren!

> Liebe Klassenkameraden!

### Introduction

An opinion speech has a creative introduction that should at least mention the author and the title of the source text. However, not all data of the source text has to appear in the introduction. For example, the date or place of publication is not required. Overall, the introduction should comprise about 50 words.

### Main part

First, summarise the topic of the opinion speech. This beginning of the main part is important so that everyone in the audience knows the necessary information about the topic.

Then mention a few arguments (one paragraph each) and justify them in detail. As help, there are various [speech structure models](#speech-structure-models) to make the opinion speech self-contained and fluent.

Address the audience and link your examples to typical everyday life so that the listeners can relate to these situations and support your opinion.

> Sie wissen das ja auch.  
> Jeder kennt den Moment, wenn der Toast vom Tellerrand kippt und man weiß bereits vorher, dass er 100%ig auf der Marmeladenseite aufklatschen wird.

Throughout the main part, your language should emphasise that the arguments are your own opinion.

#### Speech structure models

##### AIDA formula

| Abbreviation | Content                                                                                            |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Attention | Create an emotional connection, for example by telling something outrageous.                          |
| Interest  | Arouse the audience's interest by telling the content of the argument in more detail                  |
| Desire    | Present the desired target state.                                                                     |
| Action    | Suggest measures to achieve this desired state.                                                       |

##### ABBA principle

| Abbreviation (German)     | Content                                                                                       |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Anfang (beginning)        | The starting point of the speech, which is meant to win the audience's attention.             |
| Behauptung (claim) or hypothesis | The clear statement or assumption that is represented in the speech.                   |
| Belege (evidence)         | Facts, arguments or examples that support the claim and are meant to convince.                |
| Abschluss (conclusion)    | A summarising part that ends the speech and leaves a lasting message.                         |

##### AAAA principle

| Abbreviation (German) | Content                                                                                   |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Aufhänger (hook)      | The introductory part that arouses interest and captivates the listeners.                 |
| Anekdote (anecdote)   | A short, personal story or episode that brings the topic to life.                         |
| Ansprache (address)   | The main part of the speech, in which the actual message or information is conveyed.      |
| Abschluss (conclusion) | The final part that rounds off the speech, offers a summary or calls for action.         |

##### Three times formula

Divide your main part into three different segments that should answer these questions. See the questions as a guideline.

-   **Background**: How did people behave in the past? What was the situation like before?
-   **Current situation**: What has actually changed? What is the situation like now?
-   **Goals for the future**: What will change? Can the situation be improved in the future?

This model cannot always be applied, since these comparisons do not exist, especially for topics that have only recently emerged.

##### Plus-minus formula

With the plus-minus formula, you contrast the advantages and disadvantages using pro and contra arguments in such a way that your own concern takes effect.

### Conclusion

Summarise your opinion briefly once more and appeal with suggested solutions that should apply to all arguments.

### Closing

Just like in a presentation, you thank the audience in the closing.

> Danke für die Aufmerksamkeit!
