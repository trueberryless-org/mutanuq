The summary is a neutral, objective text type written in the present tense and the present perfect.

## Characteristics

A summary has the following characteristics:

-   no opinion
-   only summarise the most essential information
-   put it in your own words
-   no conclusion
-   do not address the reader
-   stick exactly to the word count
-   heading (optional): `Zusammenfassung von ...`
-   short to medium-length text type

## Structure

### Heading

The heading consists of `Zusammenfassung von` (summary of) and the title of the source text and is optional.

### Introduction

In the introduction of a summary, you mention the meta information about the source text. It should contain at least the title, author, place and date of publication as well as the topic. Because of the heading, you do not necessarily have to mention the title a second time.

It is best to use the basic sentence (Basissatz), which is demonstrated here with an example:

> Der Artikel „grüne Wolle“ von Arthur Wilderöm, welcher am 26.10.2023 in „Der Standard“ erschienen ist, beschäftigt sich mit dem Thema Umweltbelastung in Bezug auf Polyester.

### Main part

In the main part of a summary, the most important positions, information and facts of the source text are summarised in your own words. The order of the information does not have to follow the source text. Ideally, deal with one fact or argument in detail per paragraph. However, you do not have to argue yourself; rather, you should take over the content of the arguments from the source text.

To be able to extract the most important information easily, read through the source text and only mark the passages whose content you really want to include in the summary. Aim to have marked at most **four passages** in total after reading. Keep in mind that the specified word count must not be exceeded.
