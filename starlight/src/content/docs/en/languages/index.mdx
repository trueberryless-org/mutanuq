Even at a technical school such as the HTL, German and English are Matura (school-leaving exam) subjects. In both subjects, the written exam requires certain **text types**, each of which has a fixed structure and typical phrases. If you know them, you can fully concentrate on the content during the exam.

## What to expect

- **Text types (German):** Stylistic devices as well as the structure and characteristics of the summary, letter to the editor, discussion essay, commentary, text analysis, text interpretation and opinion speech.
- **Literature:** The epochs of Impressionism and Expressionism, Dadaism and the Vienna Group with their most important authors and works.
- **Text types (English):** Structure and useful phrases for articles, blogs, emails, leaflets and reports.
