A **project** is a one-time undertaking with a clear goal, a defined start and end, and limited resources – for example developing an app, rebuilding a network or the diploma thesis at the HTL. Project management covers all the tasks required to plan, carry out, control and successfully complete such an undertaking.

## What to expect

- **Magic triangle:** Why scope, time and cost always depend on each other.
- **Project controlling:** How to monitor project progress and take countermeasures in time using tools such as target/actual comparisons or earned value analysis.
