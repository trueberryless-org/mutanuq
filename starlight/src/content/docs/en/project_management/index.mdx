A **project** is a one-time undertaking with a clear goal, a defined start and end, and limited resources, for example developing an app, rebuilding a network or the diploma thesis at technical college. Project management covers all the tasks required to plan, carry out, control and successfully complete such an undertaking.

At technical college, project management accompanies you from the third year to the diploma thesis: first with objectives, context and creativity techniques, later with schedules, resource and cost plans, conflict and crisis management, all the way to project controlling and quality management.
