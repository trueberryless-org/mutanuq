Software development is the core subject of computer science at the HTL. In this section, you will learn how to build programs that do not just run, but stay **understandable**, **extensible** and **maintainable** – even when many people work on them over several years.

The examples on these pages are written in **C#**, but can be transferred to Java or other object-oriented languages with only minor changes.

## What to expect

- **Object-oriented programming:** Classes, objects and the four pillars of OOP (encapsulation, abstraction, inheritance and polymorphism).
- **Software metrics:** Quality attributes such as scalability, transparency, coupling and cohesion, measurable metrics and the SOLID principles.
- **Design patterns:** Reusable solutions to common design problems, divided into creational, structural and behavioral patterns.
